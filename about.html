Two organizations, one mission

Medicine has no borders. Neither do we.

Hatzolah Air began in 2019 as a small group of volunteer physicians, nurses and pilots who believed no one should be out of reach of the right hospital. Today that idea runs as two sister organizations — Hatzolah Emergency Air Response Team in the United States and Hatzolah Air Israel — coordinating medical air transport on aircraft ranging from light jets to long-range air ambulances.

No one should be out of reach of the right hospital because of where they happen to be.

That conviction is what turned a small group of volunteers into a standing medical air response network — built from the start as a nonprofit, charitable mission, not a business.

Our story

From a group of volunteers to a mission network.

1

2019 — Founded

Hatzolah Air was founded by Eli Rowe, Yaakov Gade and Dr. Avishai Neuman, together with a group of volunteer physicians, nurses, paramedics and pilots, built from day one as a nonprofit, volunteer-driven mission.

2

2022 — Answering the call

When war broke out in Ukraine, Hatzolah Air flew a series of medical relief and medevac missions, moving patients, medical crews and equipment between Ukraine, Moldova, Romania and Israel.

3

Today — Two sister organizations

Hatzolah Emergency Air Response Team (a U.S. 501(c)(3)) and Hatzolah Air Israel (RA) now work side by side, combining a U.S.-based medical and donor network with an operational base in Israel to coordinate missions worldwide.

Our vision

What we're working toward.

Every mission, donor conversation and hour of volunteer time is aimed at the same handful of goals.

✓

Be an immediate medical rescue and response force for the Jewish people, anywhere in the world.

✓

Carry out humanitarian missions in response to international disasters.

✓

Fly charity and medical missions even when no funding source is available.

✓

Help anyone in life-threatening danger reach the best possible care.

Looking ahead

Growing where the need is.

Hatzolah Air continues to expand its operational footprint and partnerships — deepening the U.S.-Israel base network, broadening the volunteer medical and aviation team, and building relationships with receiving hospitals worldwide so more patients can reach the care they need, faster.

Leadership

The people behind the missions.

A wider team of volunteer physicians, nurses, paramedics, pilots and coordinators makes every mission possible. Here are a few of the people leading that effort.

Eli Rowe, CMEL, EMT-P

Founder & President, Hatzolah Air

Eli is a licensed pilot, highly-experienced paramedic, and the founder and president of Hatzolah Air. His passion for emergency medicine and helping those in need are apparent and infectious, and have facilitated him building Hatzolah Air from an idea to a full-fledged air rescue organization in just a few years. On a daily basis, Eli is busy organizing, staffing, and flying Hatzolah Air missions. In his spare time, he can be found fundraising for Hatzolah Air and running his successful Health IT company, WOMBA.

Aaron Adler, MD, LLB, MBA, EMT-P

Director General & Medical Director, Israel

Aaron leads Hatzolah Air Israel's operations and medical oversight, serves as lead physician on international transport missions, and is Hatzolah Air's Israel/Middle East Director. A physician and emergency medicine resident at Hadassah Medical Center (MD, Hebrew University of Jerusalem, where he also earned a BSc in Biomedical Sciences) and a paramedic with Magen David Adom since 2009, he also holds a law degree (LLB) and an MBA in health care management from Ono Academic College, and practised law before joining Hatzolah Air. Board Member, Hatzolah Air Israel (R.A.).

Avishai Neuman, MD

Medical Director & Co-Founder

Avishai is a board-certified anesthesiologist, the Director of Anesthesia at Centurion Anesthesia, and the Medical Director at Hatzolah Air. Avishai is responsible for everything medical at HA and has the final approval on every medical flight. In addition to his role as Medical Director, Avishai takes an active role as an Executive Board Member of Hatzolah Air and is involved in many decisions on a daily, often hourly basis. One of Hatzolah Air's original co-founders, alongside Eli Rowe and Yaakov Gade. Board Member, Hatzolah Air Israel (R.A.).

Avigail Eisenstadt, NREMT

Chief Operating Officer

Avigail is a NREMT and serves as the Chief Operating Officer of Hatzolah Air, where she oversees all day-to-day operations, ensuring a smooth process all around. Avigail demonstrates her unwavering commitment to excellence in every aspect of her leadership role. A graduate of NY School of Interior Design, Avigail is the Founder & Principal Designer of AE Design Group, a WMBE-certified firm. With over a decade of experience, Avigail excels in commercial, healthcare, hospitality & multi-family design, leading innovative projects globally. In addition, Avigail is the CEO of Design Source Group, a procurement company specializing in FF&E logistics. Avigail actively supports her community of Manhattan Beach, Brooklyn. Board Member, Hatzolah Air Israel (R.A.).

Eli Zenilman

Senior Flight Paramedic · Logistics & Volunteer Director, Hatzolah Air Israel

Eli has served as a paramedic with Magen David Adom since 2015 and is a senior paramedic instructor. At Hatzolah Air Israel he is a senior flight paramedic and the Logistics & Volunteer Director, bringing extensive experience in flight operations and coordinating individual flight missions. He is also a student in the combined MD and biomedical sciences program at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

Itai Orion

Deputy Director General & Director of Operations, Hatzolah Air Israel

Itai has been a paramedic since 2012 and is a senior paramedic instructor. He previously worked in Magen David Adom's International Department in development and international coordination. At Hatzolah Air Israel he is a senior flight paramedic and serves as Deputy Director General and Director of Operations, overseeing everything operational: planning missions, coordinating ground and air teams, staffing and resources. He is also a point of contact for patient families throughout its missions.
