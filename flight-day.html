About the day

Minutes in the air. A lasting sense of what's at stake.

Flight Day is an opportunity for our closest friends and supporters to take a short flight and understand the need, the value and the importance of the Jerusalem helicopter.

✈

A quick tour

A short flight over Jerusalem and the surrounding region, departing from and returning to our landing pad at Herzog Medical Center.

⚡

Power & agility

Feel the speed and manoeuvrability that let the helicopter reach far-off scenes in a short time — where every minute counts.

✚

Meet the mission

See the aircraft and meet the people behind it, and hear how your support keeps it ready to respond.
