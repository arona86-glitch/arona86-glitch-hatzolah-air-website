Good to know

Card transaction limit

For card security and processing reasons, some card donations may be limited to approximately ₪50,000 per transaction. Larger gifts can be split into multiple transactions or made by bank transfer, including directly to Hatzolah Air.

Other countries

America Gives / IsraelGives may also offer tax-receipted giving routes in other countries. Availability can change; contact us for the appropriate route.
