Privacy Policy

How Hatzolah Air collects and uses information submitted through this website.

Draft policy — pending legal review. This page describes, in plain terms, how Hatzolah Air handles information submitted through this public website. It has not yet been reviewed by counsel and should be treated as a placeholder until final legal language is confirmed before public launch.

Scope of this policy

This policy covers the public Hatzolah Air website (hatzolahair.org.il) and its contact form. It does not cover the secure mission-intake CRM used to submit and manage flight requests — patient and medical information submitted there is handled under separate, stricter safeguards described at the point of intake.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: the name, email address and message you submit.
  • Technical/log data: standard web server and hosting logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited), collected automatically by our hosting provider for security and reliability purposes.

How we use this information

We use contact form submissions to respond to your inquiry. We use technical log data to operate, secure and troubleshoot the website. We do not sell, rent or use this information for advertising.

Medical and patient information

If you are submitting a flight request, you will be directed to our secure mission-intake system rather than this website's contact form. That system, and the medical and patient information submitted through it, is governed by separate safeguards appropriate to sensitive medical data, consistent with applicable privacy law (including the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law) in the jurisdictions where Hatzolah Air operates.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It may use essential technical cookies required for the site to function correctly.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your information. We may share it with service providers who help us operate the website and respond to inquiries (for example, our email delivery provider), solely for that purpose.

Data retention

We retain contact form submissions only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and for legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you've submitted to us by contacting info@hatzolahair.org.il.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information submitted through this website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date below reflects the most recent update.
