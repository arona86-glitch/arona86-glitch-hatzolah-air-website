About the day

Minutes in the air. A lasting sense of what's at stake.

Flight Day is an opportunity for our closest friends and supporters to take a short flight and understand the need, the value and the importance of the Jerusalem helicopter.

Lift off from our landing pad at Herzog Hospital for a quick tour over Jerusalem and the surrounding region — and feel for yourself how fast this helicopter reaches the people who need it.

The mission

When every minute counts, the helicopter brings the team to the patient.

Reserve your seats

Register your group

One registration per group — we only need contact details for one person. Pick a flight time, add your passengers and upload their passports on our secure form. Your seats are held as soon as you submit, and we'll email you to confirm.

Open the secure registration form →

Have ready for each passenger

  • First, middle and last name, exactly as on their passport
  • Age and weight (for the aircraft's load plan)
  • A photo or scan of their passport photo page
  • Up to 7 passengers per flight
